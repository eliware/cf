__EXAMPLES__
What are scopes?

Scopes are permissions Cloudflare grants to this profile. Only selected modules and their required scopes are enabled.

How do I enable another module?

Run cf auth login --scopes scope.one,scope.two or use the browser picker.

Can I use the raw API?

Yes. Log in with the additional scopes required by the endpoint, then use cf api.
